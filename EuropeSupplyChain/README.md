# Europe Supply Chain Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of **Power BI and data analytics to analyze European supply chain operations and performance**.

The project focuses on transforming supply chain data into meaningful business insights that can support operational monitoring, performance analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze supply chain performance across Europe
- Identify operational trends and patterns
- Monitor key supply chain metrics
- Analyze geographic and business performance
- Identify potential areas for process improvement
- Transform raw supply chain data into actionable insights
- Present findings through clear business intelligence reporting

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV**
- **Power Query**
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**
- **Data Modeling**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Business Intelligence**
- **Supply Chain Analytics**

## Analysis Workflow

**Raw Supply Chain Data → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Data Modeling → Analysis → Visualization → Business Insights**

This workflow demonstrates the process of converting operational data into structured information that can support business and supply chain decision-making.

## Key Analysis Areas

The project explores areas such as:

- Supply chain performance
- Geographic performance
- Operational trends
- Product and business performance
- Supply chain efficiency
- KPI monitoring

- Business intelligence reporting
